Martin Schwartz

Postdoctoral researcher · Forest remote sensing & deep learning

martin.schwartz@lsce.ipsl.fr · Paris-Saclay, France & Copenhagen, Denmark · Google Scholar · ORCID

Postdoctoral researcher in the biomass team (BIOGEO group) at LSCE, Paris-Saclay, and in the TreeSense group at the University of Copenhagen, assessing forest carbon stocks by combining satellite data (Sentinel-1/2, GEDI, LiDAR, SPOT 6/7) with deep learning to map forest height, wood volume, biomass and their changes.

    • Forest growth and disturbances using very-high-resolution data and deep learning models.
    • Supervision of doctoral students; co-organisation of the LSCE biomass team, team retreats and working seminars.
  • 2020–2023 Doctoral Researcher (PhD), Laboratoire des Sciences du Climat et de l'Environnement (LSCE) / Université Paris-Saclay France
    • High-resolution mapping of the height and biomass of French forests using satellite remote sensing and deep learning.
    • Co-supervision of J. Pauls’ master’s thesis (University of Münster).
  • 2020 Research Internship (Modeling), LSCE France
    • Study of agricultural practices to increase carbon stocks in cultivated soils using the CENTURY model.
